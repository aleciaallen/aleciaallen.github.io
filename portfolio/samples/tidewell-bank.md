Tidewell wanted to sound human without sounding like it was trying too hard. The guide gives sixty writers one simple test: would you say this to a friend across the table?

## Three rules from the guide

1. Say the number first, then explain it.
2. If a sentence needs a footnote, rewrite the sentence.
3. Warm is fine. Cute isn't. Money is serious to the person reading.
