Fernhill's regulars were loyal, opinionated and deeply suspicious of decaf. The job was to make the new blend feel like a choice, not a compromise.

## The idea

People don't love coffee only for the caffeine. They love the grinding, the smell, the warm mug at 8 p.m. So the campaign sold the ritual and treated sleep as the bonus.

> All of the ritual. None of the 3 a.m. ceiling staring.

## Where it ran

- Front of bag, plus a back-of-bag story about the bean
- In-store chalkboards with a new line each week
- Two short emails to the loyalty list

## What happened

The first roast sold out in nine days, and the chalkboard lines started showing up on customers' Instagram.
